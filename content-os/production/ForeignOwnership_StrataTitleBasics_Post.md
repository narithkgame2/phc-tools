Can foreigners actually own property in Cambodia? Yes — but the details matter more than most listings tell you.

Here's what's actually true:
✅ Land ownership (freehold) is reserved for Cambodian nationals — foreigners cannot own land outright, full stop.
✅ What foreigners CAN own outright: strata-title condominium units — this is full legal ownership, registered in your name, not a lease or a workaround.
✅ Not every unit in a building automatically qualifies for foreign ownership — each project has its own foreign-ownership quota, and that's part of what we check before listing it.
✅ "Strata-title" is a legal registration status, not a marketing term — a building being called a "condo" doesn't automatically mean this is locked in.

Here's what we do differently: we confirm a project's strata-title status before it ever goes on our list — before pricing, before floor plans, before anything gets shown to a client.

Save this if you're early in your research — it's the first legal question almost every foreign buyer should be asking.
