## 5. Is It Safe to Buy Off-Plan Property in Cambodia?
**Pillar:** Myth-busting / FAQ · **Priority:** P2
Risk-first content — mandatory opening for JP investor trust. Cover: developer insolvency risk (how to verify track record), construction delay risk, title transfer risk, exit liquidity risk, CGT 2027 implications. More risk disclosure = more trust for this audience.

## 6. New Condo Launches vs Completed Condos: Which Is Better?
**Pillar:** Myth-busting / FAQ (comparison) · **Priority:** P2
Off-plan: lower entry price, appreciation potential, delivery risk. Completed: immediate rental income, visible quality, higher entry price. Present as objective analysis, recommend by investor profile (capital growth vs yield focus).

## 7. Why Buying From a Developer With a Proven Track Record Matters
**Pillar:** PHC's verification process · **Priority:** P2
What "track record" means in Cambodia (completed projects, title delivery, resale data). Red flags to avoid: no completed projects, unclear title history. Positions PHC as the guide that only recommends verified developers.

## 8. How to Calculate Condo ROI in Cambodia
**Pillar:** Market data (financial) · **Priority:** P2
JP investors are highly numerate and want to run their own calculations. Always present **net** yield, not just gross (management fee ~8-12%, property tax 0.1%/year, vacancy factor 1-2 months/year, CGT on exit). Include a sample calculation.

## 9. Studio vs 1-Bedroom: Which Is a Better Investment?
**Pillar:** Buying process (first-purchase decision) · **Priority:** P2
Common first-purchase decision for JP investors with $80K-$150K budgets. Studio: lower entry, more competition, easier to rent in expat areas. 1BR: better exit liquidity, higher rent, longer tenant stays.
