The question we get asked more than any other: "Isn't buying property in Cambodia risky because foreigners can't really own anything?" Let's clear that up.

✅ Myth: Foreigners can't legally own property in Cambodia. Reality: foreigners can own strata-title condominium units outright — full legal ownership, registered in your name. What you can't own is land freehold.
✅ Myth: Off-plan just means trusting a rendering. Reality: off-plan is common and can be a legitimate way to buy at a lower entry price — your due diligence should focus on the developer's track record and payment structure, not the renders.
✅ Myth: You need to be in Cambodia to buy. Reality: most of the process can be managed remotely with the right advisor handling the on-the-ground parts.
✅ Myth: A guaranteed return is a sign of a good deal. Reality: no legitimate advisor should promise a guaranteed return — ask for the data behind any number you're given.

The ownership myth is the one we correct most often — it usually comes from someone half-remembering a rule about land, not condos.
