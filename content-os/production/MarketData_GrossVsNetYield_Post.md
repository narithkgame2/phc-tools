Everyone quotes Cambodia property yields — almost no one tells you gross from net, or why that gap matters.

✅ Gross rental yields across the Phnom Penh market broadly range 6–8% — that's before management fees, vacancy periods, and maintenance costs are factored in.
✅ Cambodia is a fully USD-denominated market. If you're earning or saving in dollars, there's no currency conversion risk sitting between your money and your purchase.
✅ Yields vary by district — BKK1 commands premium pricing but also premium rental demand from the expat and corporate market; newer districts are still establishing their own track record.
✅ Be cautious of anyone quoting a single guaranteed percentage for a specific project — legitimate advisors talk in ranges, because that's what the data actually supports.

When a client asks us for a yield number, we ask what management structure they're picturing first — self-managed, third-party managed, and short-term rental produce very different net numbers off the same gross figure.

Save this if you're comparing projects — the gross-vs-net gap is where a lot of "good deals" quietly stop looking so good.
