You're wiring money for a property you can't walk past to check on. How do you actually stay in control of the process from another country?

✅ Staged payments work in your favor here — because each installment is tied to a construction milestone, you can ask for progress evidence before each payment goes out.
✅ International wire transfers for property purchases are routine in Cambodia's market — but always confirm you're paying into a legitimate escrow or developer account, never a personal one.
✅ Someone should be physically checking progress on your behalf between payments — marketing photos from a developer aren't the same as an independent check.
✅ At handover, your strata-title registration should be confirmed and in your name before final payment clears — not promised as something to sort out "later."

We've walked overseas clients through payment stages while they were literally on the other side of the world during a construction milestone — that's the exact gap between "interested" and "confident" we exist to close.
