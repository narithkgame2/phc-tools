Not every project we look at makes it onto our list. Here's what we're actually checking before one does.

✅ Strata-title status — verified with documentation, not taken on the developer's word.
✅ Developer track record — have they completed and delivered projects before, as described?
✅ Payment structure — legitimate projects use staged payments tied to construction milestones, not a single upfront demand.
✅ Physical progress — for off-plan projects, we check what's actually been built against what's being marketed, not just the renders.
✅ Paperwork consistency — permits, the underlying land title, and company registration all need to line up with what's being sold to buyers.

This isn't a one-time check either. We revisit a project's status periodically, not just before the first listing goes up — construction conditions and developer circumstances can shift over the life of a multi-year project, and something that checked out at launch deserves a second look before we keep recommending it.

This is the process behind every project on our list — if you want to know what we found on a specific one, just ask.
