Booking a unit in Cambodia doesn't mean paying the full price upfront — here's what the process actually looks like, step by step.

✅ Step 1 — Booking: typically 10–30% of the purchase price reserves your unit and locks in the price.
✅ Step 2 — Staged payments: the remainder is paid in installments tied to construction progress, not a fixed calendar unrelated to what's actually being built.
✅ Step 3 — Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA): the contract that legally documents your ownership terms — read it closely, and have someone who isn't the developer explain anything unclear.
✅ Step 4 — Handover: final payment is typically due at or near completion, when you receive the keys and strata-title registration is finalized in your name.

The staged structure exists to protect you as the buyer — it ties your money to actual progress. A developer asking for a large lump sum with no staging is the pattern worth questioning, not the staged structure itself.

Save this for when you're ready to move from browsing to booking — it's the roadmap most people wish they'd seen earlier.
