Why the mix changed

PHC's channels were close to 100% listing posts. That volume of listings produced roughly 2 leads across 100+ posts — not because the listings were bad, but because a listing only works on someone who's already close to ready to buy. Educational content is what builds trust with everyone else, before they're ready. That's why every two weeks now runs 7 educational posts to 3 listing posts — not the other way around.

MonTueWedThuFri
Week AEduEduEduEduListing
Week BEduEduEduListingListing

Alternate Week A and Week B. Over any two-week stretch that's exactly 7 educational : 3 listing.

Non-negotiable
Golden Rules

These apply to every piece of content PHC publishes — educational posts, listings, and video scripts alike. They don't bend for a good story or a slow week.

1
No guaranteed returns, no project-specific net yield
Only a market-wide gross range (6–8%) as context — never a promise, never net yield for one specific project.
2
Never say "delayed" or "behind schedule"
About any project, ours or a competitor's.
3
Never give out a developer's contact details
Every post ends with PHC's own contact block — never the developer's phone, Telegram, or sales office.
4
Never invent or fabricate details
Use only what's actually provided or verified. If a spec is missing, leave it out — don't estimate it.
5
Never create false urgency
No "only 2 units left" style lines unless it is genuinely, verifiably true right now.

A mix of educational and brand tags.
example — myth-busting pillar
The question we get asked more than any other: "Isn't buying property in Cambodia risky because foreigners can't really own anything?" Let's clear that up. ✅ Myth: Foreigners can't legally own property in Cambodia. Reality: foreigners can own strata-title condominium units outright — full legal ownership, registered in your name. What you can't own is land freehold. ✅ Myth: A guaranteed return is a sign of a good deal. Reality: no legitimate advisor should promise a guaranteed return — ask for the data behind any number you're given. Comment "MYTHS" if you've heard a different one you want us to fact-check.

The brand line belongs in every script
買う前に、正しく比較しましょう。 — worked in naturally, not just tacked on.
2
The full CTA block is never shortened
Japanese and English versions both exist in full — see reference_video_script_style.md.
3
Always offer 3 hook options
Never open with a generic "today I'm at the showroom" line.
4
Avoid absolute language
No "definitely recommend," "guaranteed," "highest yield," "100% safe." Use conditional framing instead — "this may suit your investment style" rather than a blanket claim.
