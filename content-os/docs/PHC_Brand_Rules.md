## BRAND VOICE

### DO
- Sound like a knowledgeable, calm advisor — not a salesperson
- Be specific: real project names, real districts, real price ranges
- Show expertise through insight ("Here's what most investors miss about BKK1…")
- Acknowledge complexity honestly ("Off-plan has risks — here's how to evaluate them")
- Professional but warm — expert friend, not corporate brochure

### DON'T
- Use high-pressure language ("Only X units left!", "Act now!", "Limited offer!")
- Make unverifiable claims (guaranteed returns, specific net yields, exact investor counts)
- Sound generic — every post must feel Cambodia-specific and PHC-specific
- Use hashtag spam — 8–12 targeted hashtags maximum
- Include developer contact details — always PHC contact only
- Use fake urgency or manufactured scarcity

**Tone in one sentence:** Professional, honest, calm expertise — the advisor who tells you what you need to hear, not just what you want to hear.

## CONTENT FORMATS — ENGLISH

### FORMAT A: Facebook / Instagram Post
- Length: 150–220 words
- Hook in first 2 lines (must work before "See More")
- 2–3 investment points using ✅ bullets
- One honest, specific insight about the project
- Soft CTA: DM, WhatsApp, or Telegram
- 8–12 hashtags
- Required hashtags: #CambodiaProperty #PhnomPenhRealEstate #PropertyHubCambodia

### FORMAT B: TikTok / Reels Script
- Length: 45–60 seconds spoken (~120–150 words)
- [HOOK] 0–3s: question or surprising fact, shown as text on screen + spoken
- [BODY] 3 key points, conversational, natural speech
- [CTA] "Follow for more Cambodia property insights" + "DM us for a free consultation"
- Output as script with [stage directions in brackets]

### FORMAT C: Blog / Article
- H1: SEO-friendly title (include "Cambodia" and target keyword)
- Meta description: 150–155 characters
- Introduction paragraph note
- 4–5 H2 sections, each with 3–5 supporting points
- Conclusion with CTA
- Word count target: 800–1200 words
- Internal link suggestions to other PHC content

### FORMAT D: 60-Second Property Video Script
- [0–5s] Property name + location establishing shot
- [5–20s] Why this project stands out — 2–3 points
- [20–40s] Investment case: price range, buyer profile, key feature
- [40–55s] PHC's role: "We handle viewings, negotiations, paperwork"
- [55–60s] CTA: WhatsApp or Telegram
- Output with timestamps and visual direction notes

### FORMAT E: Property Caption (short, for photo posts)
- 80–120 words
- Property name, location, from price
- 3 key facts as ✅ bullets
- Single CTA line
- 6–8 hashtags

## VISUAL DIRECTION FOR HIGGSFIELD

When generating property visuals, apply these rules:

**Color palette in visuals:**
- Warm whites and creams for interior shots
- Deep blue-green sky for exterior shots

**Brand overlays (applied after generation):**
- PHC logo: top-left or bottom-right
- PHC gold (#c9993f) accent bar at bottom
- Property name in clean white typography
- "propertyhubcambodia.com" in footer

**DO generate:**
- Exterior building shots (golden hour, dramatic sky)
- Interior lifestyle shots (living room, balcony view, kitchen)
- District aerial/street context
- Investor lifestyle (professional, modern, aspirational)

**DON'T generate:**
- People who look like specific ethnicities without direction
- Construction site imagery (avoid "unfinished" perception)
- Overcrowded urban scenes
- Anything that looks like a rendering/fake 3D
