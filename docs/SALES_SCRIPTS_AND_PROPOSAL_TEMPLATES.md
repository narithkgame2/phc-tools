# PHC Sales Scripts and Client Proposal Templates

Official client consultation framework, risk disclosure profiles, and closing execution standards for Property Hub Cambodia (PHC).

---

## 1. Step 2 Journey: The Three-Option Portfolio Comparison

When presenting comparative options to clients, options must always be presented objectively using architectural and financial facts. 
All manipulative framing jargon (including "decoy", "anchor", or "irresistible") is strictly eliminated from all client facing communications.

Options are objectively categorized into three distinct tiers:

### Option 1 · Entry (Smart Capital Entry)
* **Purpose:** The lowest capital threshold into the building or district.
* **Client Profile:** First-time buyers, yield focused investors seeking minimum capital outlay.
* **Example (TS8):** Unit 45AO (Standard 1-Bed Duplex, 73 sqm gross, $101,990 net, $1,020/month).

### Option 2 · Mid (Core Balanced Benchmark)
* **Purpose:** The optimal equilibrium between living space, floor elevation, and total capital commitment.
* **Client Profile:** Long-term hold investors and future end-occupants seeking balanced tenant appeal.
* **Example (TS8):** Unit 41C2 (Premium 1-Bed Duplex, 85 sqm gross, $108,402 net, $1,084/month).

### Option 3 · Flagship (Premier Elevation & Spec)
* **Purpose:** The top architectural tier within the development, featuring maximum volume and superior orientation.
* **Client Profile:** Capital preservation buyers and high-net-worth investors seeking premium landmark assets.
* **Example (TS8):** Unit 45AA (Standard 4-Bed Penthouse Duplex, 172 sqm gross, $245,157 net, $2,452/month).

## 2. Client Persona Gating

Consultation dialogues must match client risk expectations and cultural investment criteria:

### Persona A: Local and Overseas Cambodian Clients (Budget-Flexible)
* **Engagement Approach:** 3-Option comparative tier evaluation (Entry vs Mid vs Flagship).
* **Focus:** Total capital commitment, flexible monthly payment cash flow, and room layout efficiency.
* **Dialogue Style:** Direct, consultative, and focused on monthly installment budgeting.

### Persona B: Japanese and European Clients (Risk-First, Non-Pushy Advisory)
* **Engagement Approach:** Rigorous due diligence first. Never push listings before establishing risk parameters.
* **Mandatory Disclosure Order (Strict sequence):**
  1. **Exit Liquidity:** Secondary strata resale market mechanics, typical transaction timelines in Phnom Penh, and domestic versus foreign buyer absorption.
  2. **Net Rental Reality:** Gross yields versus true net yields after deducting property management fees (typically 10% to 12%), HOA building maintenance fees, vacancy allowances, and utility reserve sinking funds.
  3. **Developer Record:** Verified developer completion history, past delivered projects on schedule (e.g. Megakim World Corp: Time Square 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7), and construction quality standards.
  4. **Market Volatility:** National economic exposure, US Dollar clearing stability, regional supply pipeline additions, and interest rate macro environments.

## 4. Closing Execution and Remote Purchase Standards

### Remote Acquisition Legal Requirements
* Notice: The developer requires hand-signed original agreements. Electronic signatures are not accepted under Cambodian real estate transfer procedures.
* Courier Handling: Original contracts are dispatched via international courier (such as DHL) with counter-signing by developer executive authorities.
* Power of Attorney: Legal proxies are available if notarized, but direct physical courier of original agreements remains the standard path.

### Execution Checklist and Verification Placeholders

```
⸻

Contract Execution Details

▫️ The developer requires hand-signed original agreements.

• Booking Receipt: $[BOOKING_AMOUNT] USD (Pending receipt confirmation)
• Developer Beneficiary: [DEVELOPER_LEGAL_ENTITY] (⚠️ verify before sending: account name must match developer entity)
• Countersigning Party: Developer authorized signatory
• Courier Tracking: [DHL_TRACKING_NUMBER / Pending dispatch]
```

### Critical Beneficiary Bank Verification
Before transmitting wire instructions to any buyer, the receiving account beneficiary name must be verified against the developer entity name stated in the master development approval. Funds must never be routed through third-party accounts.
